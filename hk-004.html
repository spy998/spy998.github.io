TRANSCRIPT

Deepnest
I've grown tired of the sound of skittering.
This place is full of life but clings desperately to the facade that it is not.
It is life that springs forth, existing to surprise and entrap.
A life of reaction and never action, there are depths of me that think to pity them; except every reaction towards me has been swift and merciless violence.
What must have happened to make this their nature? To exist only through a defense mechanism? I suspect they were forced into this by unfortunate circumstance.
Because this place was not always like this, I can see it in the bones underneath all the overgrowth. There is architecture, remnants of furniture, and silk. So much silk.
The only spider I've met is HER.
The creatures here do not remind me of her. Though, the bugs of the forgotten crossroads seem like me but are nothing like I am. Perhaps that's what these are to her, though, I feel that is not quite it.
Whatever haunts the bugs of this place seems to run deeper than the infection of the bugs elsewhere.
Perhaps the answer lies in where I was taken after I sat on that terrible bench. Those creatures, existing in an even deeper form of deception, or is it existence at all?
Only meant to lure unsuspecting travelers onto that bench, not even a life of reaction, but that of bait. Where I was tied up with silk amongst so many carcasses, at the top was an altar with, someone upon it.
Gazing at their form I could not help but feel a sorrow that I could not explain...
I will be returning to solve that mystery. AFTER I put those puppets out of their misery.
