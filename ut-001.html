Sans: The Short Straw



SPOILERS for Undertale


Use this for appropriate background music if you would like.

Every since I saw that viral meme about Sans testing Frisk with his attack I can't stop thinking about the way Sans knows about the other timelines.
He can't see into the other timelines, he doesn't know exactly what happens, but he knows what he would do if the worst case scenario came true.
This means that he only knows about what could happen, not what did. So genocide Sans is only aware of the concept of the happy ending of true pacifist.
That thought brought on this entry, what would Sans think about that, the Sans that has to deal with us when we decide to see what happens when we kill everyone.
He can't know exactly what happened, but he's probably aware that he's in the worst case scenario, and that must suck.
I picture him scrawling this into something with a pencil in the sanctuary right before he confronts Chara. So keep that in mind while reading.
Also it was so hard to keep the capitalization correct with this but I wanted it to be authentic, he speaks in lower case, he'd write the same.

Enjoy!
