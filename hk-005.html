TRANSCRIPT

Myla
On my way to investigate the mines I saw her again. The plague had taken hold of her.
Her eyes had dulled, the swings of her pick had last all exuberance, and her voice lacked the cheerful ring I recalled.
She was still singing though. She had said she could hear the crystals singing, now they sing through her.
The words are different, painfully slow and haunting. She had said she wanted to make up her own words, now she is doomed to repeat what is deep in those crystals.
I sit here awash with grief for her. For all I can remember, hers was the first song I had ever heard.
I wish with all my being that I could have sat and sang with her. Now her voice is worse than snuffed out, it is stolen from her...
I have decided to make new words for her...
Oh bury the dead as they walk again, bury the bugs who I once called friend.
Bury the wanderer who's journeyed so long, then bury the miner as she sings her song.
